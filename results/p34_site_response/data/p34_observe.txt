=== A. Is the Bakuzis site-ordering test confounded? ===
The builder picks 4 DIFFERENT REAL STANDS at csi quantiles. If those stands also differ in
initial density, size structure, species composition and elevation, then a site-ordering
inversion may reflect stand-to-stand differences rather than a site-response defect.

=== B. Magnitude of the gated SDImax site term (Weiskittel and Kuehne 2019) ===
Term is 222.7836/csi when ops$sdimax.site.term is TRUE, else the constant 15.6.
  csi= 8.0  site term= 27.85  (delta from the 15.6 constant = +12.25)
  csi=10.0  site term= 22.28  (delta from the 15.6 constant =  +6.68)
  csi=12.0  site term= 18.57  (delta from the 15.6 constant =  +2.97)
  csi=14.0  site term= 15.91  (delta from the 15.6 constant =  +0.31)
  csi=16.0  site term= 13.92  (delta from the 15.6 constant =  -1.68)
  csi=18.0  site term= 12.38  (delta from the 15.6 constant =  -3.22)
  csi=20.0  site term= 11.14  (delta from the 15.6 constant =  -4.46)

  Term is DECREASING in csi. Full swing csi 8 to 20 is 16.7 SDI units.
  Typical SDImax in these runs is 650 to 1300, so this term moves SDImax by roughly 1 to 2 percent
  and in the direction OPPOSITE to the pass 33 hypothesis.
