# v12.8 next steps: regional and composition-aware mortality refit

*1 July 2026. Planning outline only, no fitting done in this pass. Written from the diagnosis in
20260701_four_model_scorecard.md and the prior benchmarking arc. Plaintext math, no em dashes.*

## The problem this refit has to solve

A single relative-density (RD = SDI/SDImax) self-thinning lever cannot separate two opposite-signed
errors that show up on the same independent data: New Brunswick growing stands are under-predicted by
v12.7.1 (basal area -8.5 to -9.3 percent both jurisdictions is wrong framing; precisely, NB is -8.5 and
the recent Maine FIA growing stratum swings from v12.7.0's +11 percent over to v12.7.1's -52 percent
annual-increment under), while Quebec's mature ESI-covered edge is still over-predicted (total volume
+14.4 percent even after the v12.7.1 correction). NB and QC sit at similar RD (around 0.6) because QC's
higher absolute basal area is offset by a higher SDImax from more hardwood. RD alone cannot tell these
two stand types apart, so any single RD-indexed mortality curve that fixes one tail worsens the other.
This is confirmed directly in the existing diagnostics: ddbh_diag_by_region.csv shows ME at -14.5 percent
and NB at +7.2 percent bias in opposite directions, and ddbh_diag_by_rd.csv shows the residual is not
monotonic in RD alone (the 0.8+ bin is -19.1 percent while 0.6-0.8 is +9.5).

## What "regional and composition-aware" means concretely

Two separable terms, not one bigger single-region fudge factor:

1. **Composition-aware SDImax.** Replace the current single fixed SDImax with a stand-specific maximum
   density line that is a function of the softwood fraction (by basal area or trees per hectare) at the
   stand, following the mixed-species maximum size-density literature (Ducey and Knapp 2010; Woodall,
   Miles and Vissage stocking work; the additive/multiplicative mixed-SDI approaches). Concretely, fit
   SDImax(softwood_fraction) so that a pure-softwood stand and a pure-hardwood stand each get their own
   correct ceiling and a mixed stand gets a composition-weighted ceiling, rather than one number for all.
   This should do most of the separating work, since QC's higher hardwood content is exactly why it
   carries more absolute basal area at the same RD.
2. **Regional residual multiplier.** After the composition term absorbs what it can, fit a second, smaller
   region-specific multiplier on the remaining stand-mortality rate (NB vs QC vs ME) for whatever regional
   signal composition does not explain (climate, disturbance history, measurement protocol differences
   across the MagPlot/FIA/PSP sources). This is the belt-and-suspenders term; it should end up small if
   the composition term is doing its job, and its size is itself a diagnostic of how much is really
   regional versus compositional.

## Data to use

- **Fitting data**: the MagPlot QC+NB pairs (magp_qcnb_pairs.csv, magp_qcnb_tree_init.csv,
  magp_qcnb_tree_t2.csv) already carry per-tree species, so per-stand softwood fraction (by BA and by
  stems) is a direct derivation, no new data needed. Recent Maine FIA pairs (ME_PLOT/ME_TREE) add a third
  region and a third composition range. The existing SILC Seven Islands long-horizon pairs
  (silc_cfi_longhorizon_pairs.csv) anchor the long-horizon plateau target during fitting, not just at
  validation.
- **Held-out validation**: the same independent split already used for the four-model scorecard (in-domain
  MagPlot NB/QC plus recent Maine FIA), so v12.8's numbers are directly comparable to the scorecard already
  built. Do not fit and validate on the same stands.
- **Bakuzis target**: the recalibration target established in 20260630_bakuzis_recalibration_target.md
  (BA asymptote about 28 to 32 m2/ha for Acadian spruce-fir, plateau not runaway) stays the long-horizon
  acceptance criterion regardless of region or composition.

## Fitting approach

1. Derive per-stand softwood fraction (BA-weighted) for every fitting stand from the existing tree lists;
   no new extraction needed.
2. Fit SDImax as a function of softwood fraction using the existing self-thinning residuals
   (ddbh_diag_by_rd.csv style diagnostics, recomputed against softwood fraction instead of just region) via
   a simple monotone form (linear or a two-parameter logistic between a hardwood-stand SDImax and a
   softwood-stand SDImax); this is a one-parameter-family addition to the existing sdimort machinery, not a
   new submodel architecture.
3. Recompute RD using the new composition-aware SDImax and refit the existing two-segment mortality curve
   (onset, break, gentle slope, steep slope) once on the corrected RD, pooling NB, QC, and ME together to
   see how much of the bidirectional bias the composition correction alone removes.
4. If a regional residual remains, fit a small per-region multiplier on the mortality rate (start with two
   free parameters, NB and QC relative to ME as the reference, not a free parameter per jurisdiction
   without cause).
5. Re-run the coupled ingrowth release logic unchanged initially; revisit only if ingrowth residuals show a
   parallel composition or regional pattern once the mortality term is fixed.

## Validation plan (the acceptance test)

Both tails must improve together, not trade off:
- NB/growing-stand under-prediction (currently -8.5 to -9.3 percent BA, -52 percent annual increment on
  recent Maine FIA) should shrink toward zero.
- QC mature-edge over-prediction (currently +14.4 percent total volume) should not worsen, and ideally
  keeps improving.
- The by-stratum consistency spread (currently 24.2 for v12.7.1) should shrink further, ideally toward
  OSM's 12.5, without giving up the FVS-ACD modeling-efficiency advantage (NSE 0.55 to 0.78) that OSM
  lacks.
- Long-horizon trajectory must still plateau at 28 to 32 m2/ha (Bakuzis target), checked with a seeded
  version of the traj scripts (this session flagged that the existing traj1271-style scripts have no fixed
  random seed, which produced a reproducibility discrepancy; v12.8's validation script should fix a seed
  so the reported asymptote is reproducible on rerun).
- Score against the identical four-way harness already built (four_way_scorecard.R), adding v12.8 as a
  fifth column, so the comparison is apples to apples with the existing scorecard.

## Open questions worth a decision before starting the fit

- Softwood fraction by basal area or by stems: BA-weighted is more consistent with how RD and SDImax are
  already defined in the current code and is the recommended default.
- Whether the regional multiplier should be estimated freely per region or shrunk toward 1 with a
  penalty (a small ridge or a random effect over region) to avoid overfitting to only three regions; a
  random effect is the more defensible choice given only three levels and is the recommended default.
- Whether to also let the ingrowth-release RD center shift with composition, or leave ingrowth exactly as
  in v12.7.1 until the mortality term is validated; leaving ingrowth unchanged first is the recommended
  default so any residual pattern change is attributable to the mortality term alone.
