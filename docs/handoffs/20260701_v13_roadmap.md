# FVS-ACD v13 roadmap: continuous improvement on enhanced site productivity and expanded data

*1 July 2026. Outline for the next-generation FVS-ACD after the v12.9 refinements are finalized. The v12
line closed the 2018 gap to OSM with targeted levers (ESI v8 site driver, NSVB, coupled self-thinning,
composition-aware SDImax). v13 moves from levers on fixed equations to refitted, site-driven,
region-and-composition-aware component equations with uncertainty, benchmarked continuously. Plaintext
math, no em dashes.*

## Principle for v13

Stop compensating for one component with another. The v12 experience showed the tells: a density lever
(SDImax multiplier) was overshooting basal area to cover a height error, and the growing-stand tail was
being thinned to fix the mature tail. v13 fixes each component at its source, on a single consistent
enhanced site-productivity metric, refit on the expanded independent data, with uncertainty carried
through, and re-scored automatically on the multi-dimensional scorecard so improvement is measured, not
assumed.

## Pillar 1: Choose the site-productivity metric by a bake-off, do not assume ESI

Do not commit to ESI. The top-height diagnostic shows the ESI-driven anchor over-predicts dominant height
and the over-prediction scales with ESI (worse at higher ESI and at the Quebec edge), which is direct
evidence that ESI itself is part of the problem, not only its mapping. v13 treats the site metric as an
open choice decided by evidence:
- Site-variable bake-off. Refit the Kuehne diameter-growth and the height-growth / dominant-height anchor
  with each candidate site metric in turn, using the existing Stan framework (fvs-conus/stan/
  dg_kuehne2022*.stan, R/32_fit_dg_kuehne_*.R, including the l1site, traitsite, and full variants and the
  cspi run). Candidates, all staged on Cardinal: ESI (Final_SiteIndex_Output.tif), BGI, the biomass growth
  index and OSM's own driver (raster_layers/bgi/ME_BGI_V1.tif, bgi_by_pltcn.csv), CPSI / CSPI (the
  climate site productivity index, cspi_v3), and legacy CSI (ClimateNA_SI_m.tif). Test each alone and in
  combination, and consider trait-based and multi-covariate site terms.
- Decide by out-of-sample behavior, not fit. Score each site formulation on the independent benchmark and
  the multi-dimensional scorecard: unbiased dominant height (level and increment), unbiased volume and
  biomass, stable across the region and at the edges, and biologically sensible site ordering. Pick the
  metric (or blend) that gives the best-behaved height and increment; if BGI or CPSI beats ESI, adopt it.
- Whatever wins, use it consistently in every component that has a site term: diameter increment, height
  increment and the anchor, mortality, and ingrowth. One coherent site signal, not a different one per
  component.
- Add a climate-projected surface (for the chosen metric) as an explicit forward scenario driver, kept
  separate from the validated base. Never benchmark on the projected surface.
- Explore finer or dynamic productivity inputs (LiDAR terrain and canopy, soils, remote sensing) as the
  next enhancement to whichever metric wins, since it improves every component at once.

## Pillar 2: Refit the component equations, site-driven and region/composition-aware, in one system

Replace the fixed Kuehne 2022 increment and the ad-hoc self-thinning levers with a refitted, compatible
system on the expanded data:
- Maximum SDI from the brms site-specific posterior (brms_SDImax_site_specific.csv, ME.SDImax.tif) as the
  self-thinning asymptote, by forest type and region, with its uncertainty, not an ad-hoc multiplier.
- Density-dependent mortality refit with region and species-composition random effects (Bayesian
  hierarchical), so the growing and mature tails are separable by construction rather than by a single
  relative-density knob.
- Ingrowth refit and validated against observed recruitment (about 40 stems/ha/yr NB, 20 QC), with a
  density-release that matches observed rates by forest type; ingrowth has been the least-validated
  component and is the other half of the stem balance with mortality.
- Diameter and height increment refit or recalibrated on the expanded remeasurement data with the ESI site
  term, checked for growth-yield compatibility (increment as the derivative of yield).
- Fit as a system with shared random effects (region, ecoregion, forest type) so the components are
  mutually consistent and the stratification is estimated, not hand-drawn.

## Pillar 3: Expanded data, for both refitting and independent validation

- Full MagPlot (Quebec 12,892, New Brunswick 7,609, and the broader Canadian set) rather than the current
  in-domain subset, for both fitting and out-of-sample validation by region and forest type.
- SILC Seven Islands long-horizon CFI for the multi-decade projection check the remeasurement pairs cannot
  provide.
- Recent remeasured FIA across ME, NH, VT (not just ME), for a larger independent growing-stand set.
- NB and NS permanent sample plots for the managed-stand strata that FIA under-samples.
- Keep a strict fit-versus-test split by source and by space, and keep coordinates on Cardinal.

## Pillar 4: Uncertainty and continuous benchmarking

- Carry parameter and residual uncertainty from the Bayesian fits into prediction intervals, so v13 reports
  not just a point projection but a credible interval; this is a scorecard dimension v12 does not satisfy.
- Automate the multi-dimensional scorecard (the forestry-modeling skill reference) as a standing benchmark:
  every candidate version is scored on the same independent data against v12.5.0, the prior v12.7/12.9, and
  the latest OSM, quantitatively (bias, RMSE, modeling efficiency, PAI, by stratum) and qualitatively
  (Bakuzis, trajectory realism, transparency, uncertainty). Improvement becomes a measured gate, not a
  claim, and regressions are caught automatically.

## Sequence

1. Finalize v12.9.x (brms SDImax, ESI-anchor top-height fix, ingrowth validation, NB relaxed) and ship it
   as the operational August version.
2. v13.0: ESI-consistent recalibration of the dominant-height anchor and the site term across components,
   on the expanded data, current ESI only.
3. v13.1: Bayesian hierarchical refit of mortality and ingrowth with region and composition random effects
   and brms SDImax, validated out of sample on both tails.
4. v13.2: increment refit or recalibration with growth-yield compatibility and prediction intervals.
5. v13.3: climate-scenario driver (ESI 2030 and time-varying) as a separate forward mode, plus the finer
   productivity inputs.
Each step gated by the scorecard against the prior version and OSM.

## What this buys

A model that is unbiased on both growing and mature stands because the components are separable and
site-driven rather than compensating; that reports uncertainty; that improves continuously and measurably
as ESI and the data improve; and that stays fully transparent in R, the advantage the scorecard already
shows over OSM on precision.
