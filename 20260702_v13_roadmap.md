# FVS-ACD roadmap: near-term v12.10 levers and the v13 refit (updated)

*2 July 2026. Supersedes 20260701_v13_roadmap.md with the decisions settled this cycle: the site-metric
bake-off is done and ESI holds; the height over-prediction is diagnosed as edge extrapolation, not a metric
fault; and the strategic management modifiers are built and validated. This reorders the path into two
near-term v12.10 levers (management modifiers, local-calibration hook) ahead of the v13 component refit.
Plaintext math, no em dashes.*

## Where v12 stands

v12.9.x closed the 2018 gap to OSM on the operational domain with targeted, gated levers (ESI v8 site
driver, NSVB volume and biomass, coupled composition- and ecoregion-aware SDImax self-thinning). On the
independent in-domain MagPlot benchmark scored with a common NSVB system, OSM and FVS-ACD v12.9.x are
close; OSM's residual edge is on the heavily managed strata. Source verification (20260702_fvs_vs_osm_
source_verified.md) confirms why: OSM carries an explicit management covariate (IsManaged) in diameter
growth and FVS-ACD v12 does not.

## Near-term v12.10 levers (modifiers on fixed equations, gated, ship before the v13 refit)

These are the principled, low-risk levers that close the operational gap without a refit. Both are gated
and default OFF, so v12.10 with the gates off is identical to v12.9.x.

1. Strategic management modifiers (built, validated). Direct-effect planted (Type 2, sustained) and PCT
   (Type 1, transient decaying) diameter-growth modifiers by the multi-step method (Wang 1990) per Ch 11.
   The base equations carry the indirect structural effect; the modifier isolates the direct effect only.
   Result (20260702_management_modifier_scorecard.md): managed-stand total-volume bias -9.9 to -1.6
   percent and mean absolute bias 10.2 to 4.8 (OSM 8.3), closing the residual gap to OSM on managed
   stands; on planted stands v12.10 clearly beats OSM. Unmanaged stands untouched. This is the FVS
   analogue of OSM's IsManaged term, defensible because it follows Aaron's own book chapter rather than a
   black-box dummy. Next: refine the PCT time-since-treatment decay when a cleaner managed-remeasurement
   set is available (FIA TRTCD and STDORGCD for ME, NH, VT; NB and NS PSP and PEF treatment histories),
   and carry the management effect into mortality and ingrowth as a predictor (not a multiplier) in v13.

2. Optional local-observation calibration hook. A user-supplied scalar (or small routine) that scales
   height and diameter-growth predictions toward a local plot set, the way OSM's height model does.
   Optional and off by default, so it does not affect the base benchmark, but it gives operational users
   the same tuning OSM offers. Near-term, low-risk, matches the second OSM operational advantage.

## v13 Pillar 1: site-productivity metric (SETTLED, keep ESI)

The bake-off (20260702_site_variable_bakeoff.md) is decided: keep ESI as the single site driver for both
diameter growth and the dominant-height anchor. Out of sample, ESI is the best of the four candidates for
height (the deciding component) and ties for diameter growth (all four within 0.01 modeling efficiency);
BGI and CPSI carry essentially no dominant-height signal and are the wrong scale for a height anchor, and
neither has cross-border coverage. CSI is the only viable backup. Do not relitigate the metric. Keep the
climate-projected ESI surface as a separate forward-scenario driver, never benchmarked on.

## v13 Pillar 2: height over-prediction is edge extrapolation, fix at the source

The ESI-driven anchor over-predicts dominant-height increment (about 1.9x in NB, 6.4x in QC), and the
bake-off showed no alternative metric fixes it. The cause is anchor extrapolation at the Acadian domain
edge, not a fault of ESI as a height predictor: the over-predicting stands are old and near the height
asymptote (observed increment 0.04 to 0.11 m/yr) while the anchor, extrapolated beyond its calibration
domain, sits them on the steep part of the curve. Two levers, use both:

- Domain restriction. Restrict the operational domain to the Acadian core and report the QC edge as out of
  domain, as Hennigar 2018 did by excluding QC.
- Increment-consistent anchor recalibration. Recalibrate the ESI-to-anchor mapping so the height
  INCREMENT, not only the level, is unbiased in domain, with an age or edge term so old near-asymptote
  stands stop being placed on the steep part of the curve. The durable fix is increment-consistent, not a
  per-region divisor.

## v13 Pillar 3: refit the component equations, site-driven and region/composition-aware, in one system

Replace the fixed increment and ad-hoc self-thinning levers with a refitted compatible system on expanded
data: brms site-specific maximum SDI as the self-thinning asymptote by forest type and region with
uncertainty; density-dependent mortality refit with region and composition random effects (so growing and
mature tails are separable by construction) and with the management effect embedded as a predictor;
ingrowth refit and validated against observed recruitment with a density-release; diameter and height
increment recalibrated on the expanded data with the ESI term and checked for growth-yield compatibility;
fit as a system with shared random effects so components are mutually consistent.

## v13 Pillar 4: expanded data and continuous benchmarking

Full MagPlot (QC 12,892, NB 7,609, broader Canadian), SILC Seven Islands long-horizon, recent remeasured
FIA across ME, NH, VT, NB and NS PSP for the managed strata FIA under-samples. Strict fit-versus-test
split by source and space, coordinates on Cardinal. Automate the multi-dimensional scorecard (bias, RMSE,
modeling efficiency, PAI, equivalence, Bakuzis, by stratum) as a standing gate so every version is scored
on the same independent data against v12.5.0, the prior v12.x, and OSM. Carry Bayesian parameter and
residual uncertainty into prediction intervals.

## Sequence

1. Ship v12.10 (management modifiers + local-calibration hook, gated) as the operational August version.
2. v13.0: ESI-consistent, increment-consistent recalibration of the dominant-height anchor with an age or
   edge term, plus domain restriction; current ESI only.
3. v13.1: Bayesian hierarchical refit of mortality and ingrowth with region and composition random
   effects, the management effect as a predictor, and brms SDImax; validated out of sample on both tails.
4. v13.2: increment refit with growth-yield compatibility and prediction intervals.
5. v13.3: climate-scenario ESI driver as a separate forward mode, plus finer productivity inputs.

Each step gated by the scorecard against the prior version and OSM. The near-term levers (management
modifiers, local-calibration hook) reach OSM parity on the operational domain now; the v13 refit makes the
gains structural, uncertainty-aware, and continuous.
