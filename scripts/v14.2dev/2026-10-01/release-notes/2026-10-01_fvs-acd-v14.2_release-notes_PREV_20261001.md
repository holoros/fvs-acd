# FVS-ACD (AcadianGY) 14.2 refinement: release notes for PREREG16 to PREREG36

Aaron Weiskittel, University of Maine. Draft of October 1, 2026, for the `holoros/fvs-acd` repository.

## Bottom line

From September 27 to October 1, 2026, the 14.2 refinement work ran the preregistered studies PREREG16 to PREREG36 (with PREREG28b) against the 14.1 benchmark record. **No engine file, coefficient or default of the shipped 14.1 was changed by any of them, and 14.1 remains the release.** The work produced an adopted scoring convention (volume under C1, or C2 where tags allow), an adopted scorer revision (r2, from PREREG37 on), two regional candidates that pass their benchmark gates but have not cleared a Bakuzis grid (NS v141LTfi and QC p28b_small), and named failures that locate the remaining NS and QC errors. Each verdict follows the decision rule fixed in its preregistration before any held out value was read, and most RESULTs also report an independent recomputation of their gate values.

Arms ran as rescoring of frozen outputs, as offline recomputation, or through copied runners whose new switches default to 14.1 behavior, with harness gates confirming identity first. The L and T components were not re-estimated, GT1 stays a named failure, and a "candidate" is a regional usage setting beside the NB and QC anchors, not part of any release.

## Volume scoring convention and scorer r2

The benchmark runner scores observed volume with measured height where it exists and the generic `himp` curve elsewhere, while predicted volume uses the model's own heights. PREREG16 rescored every set under C0 (as published), C1 (`himp` on both sides) and C2 (measured height on both sides for tag matched survivors). Under 14.1 the C0 over C1 gap exceeded 3% in 12 of 20 sets, led by baxter_c (+13.9%), the frozen MNAP TNC set (+10.4%) and MNAP State (+9.6%), so the preregistered rule fired. The gap partly carries model height error, since mefia_l_c, with all heights measured, shows none (0.0%). All 14.2 volume gates therefore use C1, or C2 where it exists, and height is judged separately through top height (TOPHT) and tag matched height increment. Restating the 14.1 record under C1, as PREREG16 required, changes five readings most (Table 1, full table in the PREREG16 RESULT).

PREREG35 built scorer r2 by insertion only (48 lines added, 0 removed), adding a defect gate, an exclusion check and an "established at t1" stratum row. Its identity gate failed on QC C2 alone, so no adoption verdict was drawn, and PREREG36 traced that failure to the frozen PREREG16 QC C2. The QC tag frame used there was a copy of the run frame, where TREE is a row index, so C2 had paired t1 and t2 records by row position, and only 38.6% (qc_c) and 15.6% (qc_fl) of those pairs are the same tree under the MAGPlot field tags. On qc_c, row position pairs lose more than 1 cm of DBH 29% of the time, against 0.3% for field tag pairs. Correcting to field tags moves QC C2 by 0.003 to 0.006 (screened qc_c 1.073 to 1.079), and no gate from PREREG17 to PREREG35 read QC C2, so no verdict changes. With every PREREG36 gate passing, r2 scores 14.2 gates from PREREG37 on, and the field tag values supersede the PREREG16 QC C2 entries.

## NS height form, ingrowth multiplier and IMULT

PREREG17 tested the NS ingrowth multiplier (IMULT) under threshold matching, and its stems criterion chose m* = 2.5, where E3 and E6b failed. At stem balance, QMD ran 2 to 3% low and C1 volume 1 to 2.5% high on every NS set. PREREG18 refined the grid between 1.5 and 2.25 on a held out C1 volume criterion and selected 1.5, the lower edge, where every gate other than Edge passed (ns_c fold B volume ratio 1.0074, interval 0.9969 to 1.0179). An edge value cannot be adopted by design, and since the consecutive volume criterion asks for no more ingrowth than 1.5 while stems balance near 2.5, no flat NS scalar satisfies both frame types. Maine stays at 1.0.

PREREG20 fit a flatter two segment height increment correction for NS trees 25 cm and over (b1 0.034416, b2 0.005072). Every height gate passed (ns_c fold B large tree increment ratio 1.250 under 14.1 to 0.792, ns_long_fl TOPHT bias +5.47% to -1.75%). However, E6b failed by 0.08 points on ns_fl and 0.02 points on ns_long_fl, so the form was not adopted alone. PREREG22 paired the form with IMULT 1.5 and every gate passed, which makes v141LTfi the first NS arm in the 14.2 series to pass H1, H2, S1, E3 and E6b together. The two parts are additive (every interaction term below 0.002 on the ratios), though stems still run 4 to 12% low on the held out and long frames. PREREG25 showed that the remaining small tree deficit is a plot program effect, as v141LTf under predicts NS_RS height increment by about 40% at every size while NS_OP stands show no small tree deficit. No small tree size multiplier enters 14.2.

## NS_RS and NS_OP programs and long interval checks

PREREG27 found that the long interval stem deficit is survivor loss, confirmed on held out sites. The fold B survivor ratio at 16 to 60 years was 0.868 on ns_c and 0.921 on ns_long_c. Predicted annual survival sits at 0.987 to 0.991 throughout, whereas observed survival rises from 0.980 to 0.983 at 5 years to 0.992 to 1.000 beyond 20 years. Ingrowth cannot reach that term (IMULT 1.75 moved the survivor ratio by at most 0.007), so flat IMULT 1.5 stays and NS survivor mortality over multi year steps is named for a later track. These notes use revision 2 of the PREREG27 RESULT.

PREREG29 refit the PREREG20 form on NS_OP alone, and the refit failed A1 and A2, so the pooled form stays. No production stand state removes the NS_RS deficit on held out sites, since the best quartile of any variable reaches only 0.82. The programs differ instead in plot size (100 to 455 m² against a fixed 400 m²), diameter floor, height subsampling and composition, and NS_RS is named as outside the 14.2 NS height calibration.

PREREG30 checked end of interval height on the long NS records. Every gate passed on ns_long_c fold B (pooled level ratio 1.0152 under 14.1 to 1.0041 under v141LTf, 1.1194 to 1.0734 at 20 years and over, TOPHT bias +2.37% to +0.41%). The longest intervals are 35 years (ns_long_c) and 40 years (ns_long_fl), so the form is supported over that span and the PREREG26 top height loss is read as a synthetic start artifact, although its size at 100 years is not tested. The one exception, the ns_long_fl 10 to 19 year bin (0.9353 for 14.1, 0.9186 for v141LTf), was resolved by PREREG34 as a program effect. That bin is 62% NS_RS trees, program composition carries a share of 0.556 of the gap, and on its NS_OP stands the form moves the level from 1.0197 to 0.9964, as it does on ns_long_c.

## QC height anchor family

PREREG19 scored QC_AFAC from 0.030 to 1.000 under C1. The screened qc_c fold A C1 ratio spanned only 0.0067 across the grid, below the 0.010 threshold, so the anchor is a height calibration and the QC volume excess sits in diameters and stems (stems about 24% low on fold B at every anchor). These notes use revision 2 of that RESULT, which withdraws its C2 numbers. PREREG23 refit the anchor on tag height increment. The shipped 0.111 predicts about a tenth of observed height increment on tagged survivors (fold B pooled ratio 0.086), and the candidate 1.061 lifted the ratio to 0.828, but the 25 cm and over class moved from -0.021 to +0.099 m yr⁻¹, worse by 0.077, so one multiplier cannot serve every size. PREREG24, a QC stems and QMD diagnostic, gave tables only, and its RESULT is not on the host, so none of its numbers is cited.

PREREG28 split the anchor by size (1.265 below 25 cm, 0.030 at and above) and PREREG28b kept 0.111 for large trees. Both passed every gate on screened qc_c fold B, with pooled ratios of 0.875 and 0.883. In the 25 cm and over class PREREG28b reproduces the 0.111 arm to 5e-9 m yr⁻¹, while the PREREG28 clamp moved that class by an amount the tag data cannot distinguish from zero. Under the preregistered choice rule, the TOPHT difference (+11.64% against +11.97%, 0.33 points) was under the 0.5 point threshold, and PREREG28b became the 14.2 QC candidate. Its cost is TOPHT, which rises from +6.29% at 0.111 to +11.97%, inside the +14.63% limit. PREREG33 reassigned the anchor from current DBH each year. It kept every benchmark gate (TOPHT +11.14%), but on the Bakuzis QC grid top height at 100 years reached only 15.7 to 21.2 m against 22.6 to 27.6 m for p28b_small, because the trees that define top height pass 25 cm and drop to 0.111.

## MNAP benchmark and State height

PREREG21 explained why MNAP State model heights run tall and placed the whole gap on the model side (H_p 1.089 against H_o 0.993). At t1, 32% of trees carry no measured height and finish 21% tall at t2, while trees starting from a measured height finish 5.5% tall, because the model grows them about 2.4 times faster than the remeasurement shows. The gap splits roughly 55 to 45 between t1 imputation and survivor increment, and ingrowth contributes nothing. The study was diagnostic, and its mefia_l_c control is observed side only.

## NB height source and the empty t2 audit

PREREG32 audited the 38 scored stands admitted with an empty observed t2 list, 33 of 355 of them in nb_c. All 38 are plots remeasured with live stems, every one below the 5 cm scoring floor, so none is harvested, lost or a frame defect. Excluding them is material for nb_c only, where C1 volume barely moves (0.956 to 0.954) but stems fall from 1.187 to 1.140, as predicted ingrowth past the floor on these plots is a real over prediction. The audit kept the published admission and proposed three scorer checks, which r2 now carries. The nb_c reading of the earlier NB height source study (PREREG14 steps 1 and 2) would move only from 0.987 to 0.985 under exclusion.

## Bakuzis grids

PREREG26 ran the Bakuzis NS grid for v141LTfi against 14.1 under a noninferiority rule and did not clear it. The worst basal area site ordering margin is 8 inversions against 7, one inversion in one cell of pure balsam fir (*Abies balsamea* (L.) Mill.) at 6,000 trees ha⁻¹, which the follow up arms attribute to threshold matching with IMULT 1.5. The reported arms add a larger caution, as the height form lowers 100 year top height by 14 to 25% (balsam fir on an 18 m ESI, 16.2 m against 20.8 m under 14.1).

PREREG31 ran the Bakuzis QC grid for p28b_small and did not clear it, with four new site ordering flags and worse margins on Reineke (1.496 against 1.266) and basal area ordering (14 inversions against 13). The reference is not a credible long run projection, since under 14.1 every seeded tree keeps 0.111 for 100 years and top height at age 50 is 0.26 to 0.80 of ESI. The candidate clears 31 top height falls and every other 14.1 flag on six relations. PREREG33 failed the same grid (Reineke 1.412 against 1.266).

## Current 14.2 candidates

- **Scoring.** Volume gates under C1, or C2 with field tags, height scored separately, scorer r2 from PREREG37 on.
- **NS.** v141LTfi, the PREREG20 height form with threshold matching at IMULT 1.5. It passes the benchmark (PREREG22) and long interval height checks (PREREG30, PREREG34) but not the PREREG26 Bakuzis grid, and NS_RS is outside its calibration.
- **QC.** p28b_small, QC_AFAC 1.265 below 25 cm and 0.111 at and above. It passes the benchmark (PREREG28b) but not the PREREG31 Bakuzis grid.

By their own preregistrations, neither enters a release before a Bakuzis grid clears it.

## Open items

- Whether noninferiority to a 14.1 reference that cuts seedling cohort height growth to about a ninth is the right Bakuzis gate for QC.
- NS survivor mortality over multi year steps, or a long interval remeasurement convention (PREREG27).
- The 100 year size of the NS height form top height loss, which PREREG30 could test only to 35 years.
- The 7.0 m old harness ESI offset between ns_c and ns_long_c (PREREG29), reported for repair and not acted on.
- MNAP State t1 height imputation, large tree increment, and the unread mefia_l_c model side control (PREREG21).
- The QC stems deficit of about 24 points shared by every anchor arm (PREREG28).
- The PREREG24 RESULT and the PREREG23 and PREREG24 preregistration texts, which are not on the analysis host.
- A revision of the PREREG19 correction note, whose attribution of the 11,632 qc_c tag matches conflicts with PREREG36.
