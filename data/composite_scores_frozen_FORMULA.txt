FVS-ACD frozen composite score definition (0 to 100, higher is better)
=====================================================================
Per metric per model on a given stratum:
  bias_score   = 100 * (1 - min(1, |bias_pct| / 20))
                 (0% bias -> 100; |bias| >= 20% -> 0)
  nse_score    = 100 * max(0, NSE)
  metric_score = 0.5 * bias_score + 0.5 * nse_score
  metric_score values are frozen in composite_metric_scores_frozen.csv and
  are read, not recomputed, by every downstream composite and figure.

PRIMARY composite (volume + value weighted) = weighted mean of the nine
  metric_score values with volume and value carrying the highest weight:
    TOTVOL   3
    VALUE    3
    MERCHVOL 2
    SAWVOL   2
    BIOMASS  2
    BA       1
    SDI      1
    QMD      1
    TOPHT    1
  sum of weights = 16. This is the primary reported composite: it puts total
  volume and stumpage value first, the remaining merchantable outputs
  (merchantable volume, sawlog volume, biomass) second, and the four
  structure metrics (basal area, stand density index, quadratic mean
  diameter, top height) last.

EQUAL-WEIGHT composite (reference / robustness) = simple mean of the nine
  metric_score values (every metric weight 1). Retained as a secondary column
  to show the ranking is not an artifact of the weighting.

Strata: POOLED_indomain (esi_cov TRUE or PROV==NB); Softwood; Mixedwood; Hardwood.
bias_pct and NSE are computed on the frozen basis (frozen_subsidiary_W.rds).
