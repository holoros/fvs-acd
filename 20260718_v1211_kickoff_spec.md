## Goal

Keep the v12.10 engine. Correct the three components the decomposition isolated so the long-horizon volume
bias stops flipping sign across the stand-maturity gradient. Validate on the maturity-gradient benchmark with
a source held out, and confirm the frozen v12.10.1 in-domain FIA NSVB result and Bakuzis realism do not
regress.

## The three levers, in priority order

1. Recruitment (highest leverage). Predicted ingrowth runs 3 to 42 percent of observed on every source; the
   entire Maine FIA 20-yr under-prediction (predicted 7.6 vs observed 38.5 m3 ha-1) is this deficit. Raise the
   ingrowth rate. In the AcadianGY engine this is the recruitment step feeding make_acd_tree; the earlier
   note flagged the expected-value hurdle recruitment sitting near a floor (residual of the 2018
   too-infrequent ingrowth problem). Fit the recruitment rate against observed recruitment pooled across the
   multi-source long-interval data, keyed to density and composition.
2. Mortality and self-thinning (second mover). The MORTCAL and self-thinning lever was tuned on the Seven
   Islands CFI and over-removes off that range: predicted natural mortality is 4.6x observed at CTRN and 2.5x
   at NB, about right at Baxter and Maine FIA. Re-express it as density and structure aware (a function of
   relative density, QMD, and management or stand-origin state) rather than a SILC-anchored constant, so it
   holds correct behavior in mature natural stands while it stops over-thinning managed and high-density
   residual stands.
3. Survivor large-tree diameter increment (mature-end fix). Survivor diameter growth is too high on every
   source (basal-area survivor term +58 percent at Baxter); it surfaces as over-prediction only where there is
   no offsetting recruitment (mature Baxter). Rein the large-tree diameter increment or lower the old-tree
   asymptote. Lowest volume leverage overall but it owns the mature-reserve over-prediction.

  Baxter, SILC, Maine FIA, PEF, NB, CTRN. Hold one source out (Baxter is the natural mature-end holdout).
- Guardrails that must not regress: the frozen v12.10.1 in-domain FIA NSVB result (BA -10.07, top height

  long-horizon density plateau. Gate on all of these, byte-identical gate-off check against v12.10.1.
